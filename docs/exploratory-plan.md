# Exploratory controls at within-block correlation 0.99

This follow-up was chosen after the formal results were available. At training size 80 the original Lasso fits completely replaced the selected members of 244 of 600 jointly detected signal blocks when every block had correlation 0.99, compared with 5 of 600 at 0.9. The original selective-correlation controls used 0.9. The two new controls localize the stronger correlation to the three signal blocks or to the 21 null blocks.

Use 200 independent training pairs per control, separate folds within each training sample, and the original Lasso, Elastic Net and Ridge implementations. All coefficients, noise, penalty grids, stopping rules and thresholds follow the frozen simulation. The two controls use a new root seed and condition IDs. Count every attempted pair, report failures after the existing solver retry, and use common valid pairs. Source settings are in [the configuration](../config/exploratory-rho099.json).

Use the original size-80 independent-block and all-blocks-at-0.99 results as reference cells. For Lasso and Elastic Net tabulate the joint event over all valid pairs and low overlap conditional on close error, both with their denominators. Count complete within-block swaps among jointly detected signal blocks. Also report population RMSE, variable and group Jaccard, support size, true-variable recall, false inclusions and the signal/null components of the support symmetric difference. Ridge is a prediction baseline.

The primary descriptive comparison is the Lasso swap-rate difference between signal-only and all-blocks-high correlation. Also compare signal-only with null-only. Compute percentile intervals using 5,000 independent bootstrap draws across conditions, resampling whole training pairs inside each cell to preserve dependence among the three blocks. Report estimates and intervals without an equivalence margin. Wilson intervals for joint and conditional events use their respective pair denominators. No tuning adjustments or new test thresholds follow from these controls.
