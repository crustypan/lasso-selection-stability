# Protocol before the pilot

The primary estimand is the probability, over two independently generated and separately tuned training samples, that population RMSE differs by at most 0.05 noise SD and variable Jaccard is at most 0.5. Report both marginals and the conditional probability among close pairs with its numerator and denominator. Count double-empty sets as Jaccard 1 and report their frequency. Repeat at gaps 0.02 and 0.10. Report continuous distributions.

Use 24 independent Gaussian blocks of five standardized variables. The middle member of each of the first three blocks has coefficient one. All remaining coefficients are zero. Noise is independent N(0,1). Population signal variance is three for every correlation, oracle RMSE one and the population mean predictor RMSE two. At n=80,240 compare equicorrelation 0,0.5,0.9,0.99. At n=80 add (signal, null) block correlations (0.9,0) and (0,0.9). The prespecified endpoint contrast is 0.9 minus 0 within each n. Primary uncertainty is over training pairs, using Wilson intervals for event proportions and paired bootstrap for within-condition method contrasts.

Within each sample, use identical shuffled five folds across methods, independently across samples, fold-trained scaling and minimum validation MSE. Fit Lasso, Elastic Net (mixing fraction 0.5), and Ridge; leave the intercept unpenalized. All penalty grids use average loss. Use original-scale coefficients, population covariance and the intercept for analytic risk and cross-model prediction disagreement. Audit each main condition on 100,000 independent observations. Save supports, sizes, raw coefficients and convergence records. Separate exact nonzero support from numerical convergence checks.

Report true-variable recall, false inclusions (signal-block companions separately from null-block variables), signal/null group hits, group Jaccard and symmetric differences by block type. For each signal block distinguish neither hit, one hit, disjoint nonempty supports, partially overlapping non-identical supports and identical nonempty supports. For disjoint swaps use jointly hit blocks as denominator. Resample complete pairs for block summaries. The size/risk-matched descriptive comparison requires cross-method size difference <=2 and RMSE difference <=0.05 in each of the two samples. Report the retained denominator.

Prespecified checks: independent seeds at n=80 and correlations 0 and 0.9; independent pilot median penalty by condition and method applied to the same formal samples; n=80 AR(1) blocks with mean pairwise correlation 0.9 and signal positions unchanged. Formal repetitions target 200 per condition. Report failures and common usable pairs. Later additions are exploratory.

Credit uses UCI 350 (DOI 10.24432/C55S3H, CC BY 4.0). Use monthly status, billed amounts and repayment amounts in September through April 2005. Audit bytes, IDs, target, mapping, special codes and all 18 fields. Main encoding floors nonpositive status at zero; raw codes are a sensitivity analysis. Positive levels enter linearly on log odds. Split a fixed stratified 20% test set by customer ID. Use development data for correlations and reserve 20% of the development pool for pilot validation only. Each pair draws two disjoint stratified samples of 3,000; different pairs may share customers. L1 and elastic logistic models share stratified folds and tune by log loss. The baseline predicts the training prevalence. Record Brier score and log loss. The credit closeness threshold is max(0.001, 0.05 times median pilot baseline-minus-L1 validation log loss). Sensitivities halve/double this threshold. Formal resampling uses the full development pool; the fixed test set is scored only after freeze. Interpret results descriptively within this dataset.

Before formal fitting, Junyang reviews the design, pilot diagnostics, threshold and precision and freezes the configuration. Separate pilot and formal outputs and random streams. Only describe formal results after evaluation. Credit raw records and customer identifiers stay out of GitHub. No public release is scheduled.
